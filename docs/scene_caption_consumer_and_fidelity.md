## Native consumer

42E30–42E40 restricts route selection to 0–3. Dispatch at 42E88/42E9C/42EB0/42EC4
loads the route's image list, caption list and initialized count. In the scene
viewer loop, 42FD0 selects `caption_table[index]`; native CED28 measures the whole
NUL-terminated string. The caller computes `128 - strlen * 6 / 2`, selects the
same complete pointer again at 42FF8, and invokes 456A0 at 43008. Caption y=70
comes from initialization at 42C44–42C4C and frame field +50.

456A0 initializes a draw-local context via D5160 with owner+6028, then writes
x/y/style into +24/+28/+30 and calls D5404. Style is 1. The canonical D5404
renderer includes previously accepted changes and must be retained.

register poisoning. All 164 V137 caption entries retain complete bytes, leading
and last characters, NUL, expected x/y/style, stack and preserved registers.
Native writes are bounded to the stack. This does not execute full pixels,
navigation or gameplay. Ten source/array/native-consumer tests and Ruff pass.
