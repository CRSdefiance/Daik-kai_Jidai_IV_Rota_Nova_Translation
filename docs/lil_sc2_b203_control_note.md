# Lil SC2 B203 medicine-book control note

All 21 B203 records are translated from the clean Japanese source. Mikhail
and Lil find caterpillar fungus; an herbalist explains its medicinal use and
offers a book on medicinal ingredients. Lil proposes 1,000 coins, the
herbalist asks only 100, and the admiral's charm rises by one.

Mikhail's `4C`, Lil's `02`, the herbalist's `AA`, and notice `FE`
presentation states are preserved. The reward retains exactly one live
`FI` admiral-name macro. English prose avoids literal uppercase `I` and
`F`, which are unsafe renderer bytes.

All 21 records pass the fixed-byte, speaker, macro, wrap, pair-phase, and
first-glyph audit. Three exact-font contact sheets were visually reviewed,
including both prices and the reward placeholder. Direct saved-ROM
verification confirms every insertion. Runtime cold-boot remains necessary
to check the book handoff, macro expansion, portraits, nameplates, and
first letters.
