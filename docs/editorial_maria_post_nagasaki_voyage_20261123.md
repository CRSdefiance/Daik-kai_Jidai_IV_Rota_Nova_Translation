# Maria's voyage after Nagasaki

A cold boot loaded the existing Maria battery save, and ordinary controller input departed Nagasaki on the registered Nagasaki Dream ROM. Kurushima's fleet intercepted Maria and triggered all eight panels of the confrontation. The clean-source pass confirmed three layout problems in the rebuke, dismissal and surrender offer. The continued voyage reached the takeover of **Hua**, the withdrawal call, Assign Sailors, and the original **Abandon Ship** choice. Captures show Hua at zero crew before the choice and removed from the fleet after confirmation. All thirteen shared panels retain their wording. The terminal Chun warning led to Game Over and a return to the title.
