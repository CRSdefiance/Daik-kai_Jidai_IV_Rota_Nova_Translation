# Lil SC2 B113-B114 control mapping

B113 is the jade offering discovery. The `13` scholar state is already mapped
by Lil's route profile; the same source appears in SC0/SC1 B122 and SC3 B96.
`D6` is the lookout state established in B112. `82` and `92` begin ordinary
Japanese companion text, so their English begins directly with its first glyph.
The short B113 R0021 response omits the admiral address rather than adding a
gendered title when addressing Lil.

B114 is the townsman's cacao follow-up: reduced slave and drug smuggling, cacao
as the town specialty, and directions to inland ruins. Every townsman record
starts with `68` followed by coherent Shift-JIS prose. That printable byte is
mapped as a presentation state only in the V32 profile; it is preserved through
`{SPEAKER:68}`. `02` remains Lil's state. The familiar address is localized as
"little lady," which Lil rejects, without inventing kinship or rank.

B113 R0004 (`60 30 46 93 80 3E 63`) and R0025 (`30 48 8B A8`) are non-prose
fragments. V32 excludes both pending runtime mapping and leaves them unchanged.
All 19 spoken records passed native-box QA and preview review. The jade event,
scholar portrait, cacao branch, townsman's nameplate, first glyphs, ruin gate
unlock, and transitions still need a cold-boot sample.
