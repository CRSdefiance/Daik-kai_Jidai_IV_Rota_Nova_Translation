# Confirmed-name migration: first SC0 editorial batch

V232 is research, not a ROM. V218 remains current. The previous goal turn checked
additional publisher sources and found no faithful new Online transcript. This
turn completes fresh source/context/natural-English review for19 SC0 name-dependent
records and prepares complete offline layouts for them.

## Source and editorial corrections

The clean Japanese and actual previous/next **spoken** records are stored with
each line. The older migration's immediate neighbors often consisted only of VM
commands; those are not useful dialogue context. No command is translated or moved.
All existing speaker selectors and runtime name macros retain their exact order.

Corrections include:

- Julio personally offers to explain more actions at sea, rather than speaking
  like the heading “A few more actions at sea need explanation.”
- “No time like the present, Rafael. I'm counting on you!” restores both the
  source's prompt-action idiom and its omitted expression of trust.
- The sea-route tutorial asks whether things are going **smoothly**, not merely
  whether they are clear.
- Rafael's introductions become complete, natural sentences while preserving
  his nervous pauses and respectful address.
- Albuquerque introduces “a very promising young man.” The source does not
  add the earlier draft's “young admiral” title in that sentence.
- Silveira's question restores the topic transition and specific **naval** scope.

“Rafael, my boy...” is retained as Julio's familiar/teasing mentor address. The next
spoken line asks him to stop calling Rafael a boy, so removing the term would
break the exchange. It is not a claim of kinship. Inconsistent unverified speaker
surnames in older metadata are normalized to mapped first names **Julio** and
**Claudio**; this is not a new ROM naming decision.

Existing natural warnings/calls are retained where faithful. A passing old review
flag does not automatically approve the new migration:437 original records have
true per-record v2 gates, yet this sample still exposes clipped prose and inconsistent metadata.
The other records do not inherit approval from these19.

## Formatting and allocation

All19 complete exact-font previews on three sheets were reviewed. First/final
letters, punctuation, words and declared Rafael/Castor/full-name expansions fit
the proposed route profiles with balanced automatic wrapping. Translators add no
layout spaces or line breaks.

Literal uppercase I/F are reserved by the original expander. The research serializer
uses the existing CP932 full-width Latin glyphs only in literal text, leaving
controls/macros untouched and the editable English unchanged. This preserves
natural prose instead of dropping “I'm” or rewording a sentence to avoid a letter.
Its exact markup and proposed byte payloads are recorded; these lines' actual native
expansion/display remains a separate integration gate.

**Nine** records pass the original fixed allocations. **Ten** complete corrections
exceed their old slots. They have zero-blocker private-pool layouts using the same
calibrated source controls, name lengths, pair-phase repair and screen bounds.
This is formatting/allocation research; it does not authorize script relocation.
The existing scoped message-catalog mechanism must be extended and tested against
their exact current source keys before release. No English is shortened to make
these allocation checks pass, and the fixed-slot failures remain visible.

SC0 B141 R0107 remains excluded: the high presentation byte makes its clean export
misleading and the current dossier lacks sufficient speaker/context provenance.
Do not infer approval, discard its prefix or turn the decoder artifact into prose.
