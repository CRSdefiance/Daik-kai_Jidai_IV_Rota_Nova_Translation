## Scene decisions to carry forward

- Lil's spice exhibition is for merchants of the Indian Ocean coast, with
  sales at the exhibition. Basra does not trade in spices, and the organizer
  has nowhere to store partial loads. Preserve one hold per spice, all nine
  names, all nine conditional prompts, one shipment, 40,000 gold and the
  slight Basra share increase. Confirm the commodity names and Market Info
  against the current catalog and menu. The subsequent item is unidentified.
- Lil's bridge expedition distinguishes conjecture about the flood, a long
  upstream log-bridge detour and a 100-gold ferry toll after she proposes
  crossing on foot. The bridge/ferry choice and the elapsed day and fatigue
  report matter. Preserve the crew's differing confidence about the map.
- Sakura and Lil visit part of the route together. The temple elder tests
  humility and courtesy without evidence of religious conversion. Lil's
  insolence and exaggerated politeness are both deliberate. The 20,000 gold
  covers the shopping and errands. The best variants preserve her spontaneous
  reaction to it before she switches back to a polite form. The map guardians
  live far northeast of China. Keep those directions consistent with Hodram's.
- Maria's shipyard sequence distinguishes gunnery expertise from shipbuilding
  expertise and keeps the immediate and later-return branches. Keep the game's
  description of the carronade, the Islamic fleets' boarding advantage and the
  armor design that spreads the force of a hit. The guildmaster's uninvolved
  couriers and the old gift do not acquire new identities or uses.
- The alternate path with Xien compares a compass reading against the midday
  sun. Maria's hunch is the other option. Jam's incredulity and Xien's dry reply
  play against each other. The captain recognizes a new fork after a day passes.
