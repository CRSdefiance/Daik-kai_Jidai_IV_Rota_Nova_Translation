# Maria's crew tell the story of the admiral

Source and current command references are in the [B116 pair packet](../translations/editorial_checkpoints/palace_and_london_20261122/maria_B116_paired_source.json). Its two continuation offsets belong to R0041 and R0051. The [earlier native-page ledger](../translations/maria330_integrated_final_review_v1.json) records both physical pages for each of those lines. Bind the current block and both continuation commands before staging. These are contextual editorial decisions pending native formatting and verification on the next candidate.

The younger crew member asks why everyone fears Maria. Two replies give the same point in distinct voices. Ian then tells a story in which Maria rewards a victory and punishes desertion in the same moment. The witness reactions carry the emotional conclusion before another crew member tries to give a grander account and the others slip away. Keep the difference between a formal reward and the pension ultimately paid to the man's family.
