# Legal and safety boundaries

1. This tool operates only on a user-supplied local ROM.
2. Do not commit or distribute ROMs, extracted copyrighted assets, or rebuilt ROMs.
3. Distribute user-created work only as a binary patch where legally permitted.
4. Known clean-ROM hashes may be recorded as metadata, never the copyrighted bytes.
5. This project does not download ROMs or explain how to obtain them unlawfully.
6. Users are responsible for ownership, dumping, and local-law compliance.
