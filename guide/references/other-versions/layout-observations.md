# Initial layout observations

Reviewed 2026-10-11. This is a sample-page visual review, not a complete reading or completeness audit. Numbers below are PDF page positions, which differ from printed page numbers. Originals and checksums are in [the reference manifest](manifest.json).

## PlayStation IV Chinese strategy guide

Source: [Archive.org ps-iv](https://archive.org/details/ps-iv), local [PDF](uw4-ps-chinese-strategy-guide-1999.pdf), 215 scanned pages.

Inspected PDF pages 1, 3, 20, and 80. Page 1 is an illustrated cover; page 3 is blank. Page 20 (printed 17) combines a character illustration, biography, item notes, a compact statistics table, and an orange information box. A margin label identifies the chapter and subsection. Page 80 (printed 77) uses a strong topic heading, grayscale illustration, an in-game screenshot with caption, and narrow vertical text columns.

The catalog credits KEY and dates the book to 1999. These sampled pages do not establish its publisher or official Koei licensing. Keep that attribution question open until its publication page is examined.

## PC IV Simplified Chinese manual

Source: [Archive.org manual](https://archive.org/details/uncharted-waters-4-manual-chs), local [PDF](uw4-pc-chinese-manual.pdf), 19 scanned PDF pages, including two-page spreads.

Inspected PDF pages 1, 3, and 10. The cover spread visibly includes KOEI, a 1999 copyright, and regional distributor labels. Page 3 presents town-command explanations in columns, shaded tip boxes, and a simple share diagram. Page 10 groups a command index into shaded boxes with page references. Running footers show topics and printed page numbers.

This provides stronger visible Koei branding than the strategy-guide samples. It is an earlier PC manual; no DS controls or mechanics should be inferred from it.

## Suggested adaptations for our guide

1. **Separate reading from lookup.** Use spacious walkthrough pages for progression and compact, consistent tables for ports, items, and recruitment.
2. **Make navigation persistent.** Add protagonist, region, chapter title, and page number to each running header/footer, with clickable contents in the eventual PDF.
3. **Build reusable character entries.** Combine a concise role summary, recruitment conditions, missable warnings, and a small data table. Keep those conditions sourced and version-qualified.
4. **Give screenshots a clear job.** Pair each capture with the action or result it illustrates. DS images should retain the relationship between the two screens; design around their vertical shape.
5. **Use distinctive warning boxes.** Place irreversible-choice warnings before the relevant action. Separate them visually from optional tips and source-verification notes.
6. **Add a task index.** Help players find actions such as recruiting, buying a ship, opening settlement trade, or locating treasure without reading a full chapter.

Use horizontal English text and our own typography and artwork arrangements. The samples' layouts are inspiration, not templates to duplicate. Scan quality varies, with washed-out areas and visible page curvature; the checksum establishes download integrity, not legibility or publication accuracy.
