# Player-facing voice and provenance

User direction, 2026-10-11: write the guide for people playing the game, in the natural voice of a strategy guide. Do not make it read like documentation of the translation project. Final publication remains an illustrated PDF; the current deliverable is GameFAQs-style text.

- Explain what to do, why it helps, and what to watch out for.
- Place warnings before the affected decision. Use confident instructions when the evidence supports them; retain a concise uncertainty note when it materially affects play.
- Do not invent personal play experience, a tested route, exact thresholds, prices, coordinates, or mechanical certainty.
- Use the existing English labels without narrating the translation process in the walkthrough.
- Keep technical record IDs, file paths, verification procedures, screenshot tasks, and source disputes in research notes. Source codes and an accessible references appendix may remain in the player edition.
- Keep spoiler detail proportionate to the task: explain a destination and consequence without recounting unnecessary dialogue.
