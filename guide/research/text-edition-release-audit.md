## Retained limitations

The guide deliberately retains source conflicts and untested DS details: exact quest thresholds and recoveries where sources disagree; Maria's Macao failure conditions; Rafael's Malacca/Manila gate discrepancy; Aziza's encounter count; DS stat differences from published comparison tables; Golden Route calculation/payment details; random Mystery Hunting selection/appraisal; precise travel, cannon and refit formulas; and unavailable map/community/book content. These are documented research limits with usable source-backed instructions or conservative preparation advice, not unwritten reference chapters. No physical-DS or complete emulator playthrough was performed for this release.

No required text section is deferred to the illustrated edition. The remaining publication work is the separately requested later PDF layout with photos/screenshots. Final machine-check output and the exact released TXT hash are saved in `text-edition-release-validation.json`.
