# Rafael New World evidence and review

Recorded 2026-10-12 for R37–R46. Primary DS source: [Dekuno, 新大陸](https://dekunobou-rotanova.seesaa.net/article/48270717.html). Mixed-version corroboration: [wiki, ３．新大陸](https://w.atwiki.jp/offlinedaikoukai/pages/54.html). Both retrieved on that date. No route was run in the emulator or on hardware for this chapter.

## Existing wording and mechanical evidence

- `translations/raphael_deep_route_v52.json`, B178 R0020/R0024: each player takes 1–3 coins; taking the last loses. R0064/R0116/R0148 describe leaving a multiple of four plus one. The guide's example is independently arithmetic-consistent; it does not claim to have executed the minigame.
- The translation's R0132 contains an arithmetic inconsistency: taking three from five after the opponent takes one does not leave four. It is not used as game strategy. The original Japanese record still needs inspection to distinguish a translation defect from a character's intentionally wrong answer; neither explanation is assumed here.
- `translations/raphael_deep_route_v8.json` supplies Charles Jean Rochefort's name and recruitment context.
- `guide/research/ds-static-item-reference.json` supplies Tlaloc Knife (43), Crystal Skull (154), Pittacus Blade Map (161), Ancient City Map (172), Ceremonial Knife (180), Sun-Crest Sheath (187).
- [DS purchase list](https://dekunobou-rotanova.seesaa.net/article/29158147.html) gives Ancient City Map in Veracruz for 1,000 gold.
- [DS goods table](https://dekunobou-rotanova.seesaa.net/category/2333225-1.html) lists timber in Pernambuco and corn in Havana. Source data, not observed live prices/stock.

## Unresolved DS conditions

1. Tlaloc Knife: precise search location, triggering companions, and full guild-request flow remain to be added. Wiki influence threshold of 1,000 is explicitly mixed-version.
2. Pensacola: wiki specifies Arcadius/Janus and a Portugal-annexation gate. These are retained as conservative companion advice and a possible later story gate, not DS-confirmed conditions.
3. Sun-Crest Sheath: Dekuno ties it to taking Escante's share in Merida; wiki gives regional leadership plus departure office. Neither description is silently substituted for the other.
4. The DS author marks a Veracruz entry scene as potentially absent. It is not converted into an unconditional required trigger.
5. Combat with Maldonado/Escante: the wiki says dissolution is unnecessary for the proof, while Dekuno's story flow emphasizes fighting. The chapter records proof-object triggers without declaring either faction universally optional or mandatory on DS.
6. Proof-map exploration: use the player's own map; no coordinate was invented. Full map/search mechanics still need verification.

The optional Vels, Venice, and Cape Verde post-recruitment events listed by the DS author remain for later side-quest coverage. This chapter does not certify every optional scene or complete Rafael's full route.
