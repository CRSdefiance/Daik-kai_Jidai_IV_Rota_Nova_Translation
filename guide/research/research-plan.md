# Research rules and guide plan

## Editorial and verification rules

1. Nintendo DS is authoritative. PC, PlayStation, PSP, mobile, and HD material is comparison evidence only unless DS compatibility is established.
2. Prioritize Dekuno, then verified DS-compatible material from the other indexed sources.
3. Translate carefully, maintaining the [glossary](glossary.md) and preserving Japanese names wherever English naming is uncertain.
4. Attach an exact source URL to every important fact, event, item, coordinate, requirement, or mechanic. Use article/section/table locators where possible.
5. Separate confirmed DS behavior, DS-specific source reports, mixed-version reports, and unverified claims.
6. Record conflicting claims together; explain the evidence for any resolution.
7. Explicitly flag missables, mutually exclusive rewards, recruitment prerequisites, and irreversible decisions.
8. Never invent absent walkthroughs, events, coordinates, prices, passwords, or prerequisites.
9. Retain original Japanese terms and choice text beside uncertain translations.
10. Write original English summaries and explanations. Avoid copying whole copyrighted walkthroughs or databases; retain only necessary brief excerpts with attribution.
11. Maintain the accessible [source index](source-index.md), including newly followed child pages.
12. Mark inaccessible/incomplete sources and unverified sections. A successful page fetch does not establish that gameplay behavior was verified.

## Evidence status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| DS-confirmed | Independently demonstrated on DS software, with a recorded ROM/version and reproducible state/test |
| DS-source-reported | Explicitly DS-specific source claim, not independently demonstrated |
| Cross-platform-unverified | Other-version or mixed-version claim awaiting DS applicability checks |
| Conflicting | Sources or tests disagree; preserve each claim and its context |
| User-supplied | Lead or metadata supplied by the user but not yet independently inspected |
| Inaccessible | Required page/text could not be retrieved; no content inference permitted |

Emulator observations should name the emulator and ROM/version and be described as emulator verification. Record physical DS testing separately. For translated-ROM tests, distinguish original game behavior from translation-specific changes. Source consensus alone does not earn DS-confirmed status.

## Fact record template

Use one record per substantive claim when extraction begins:

```text
Fact ID:
Topic / guide chapter:
Japanese term or brief choice text:
English explanation:
Protagonist / region / port / facility:
Prerequisites and event order:
Trigger and exact dialogue choice:
Result / reward / cost:
Missable, irreversible, or mutually exclusive? Unknown is valid:
Coordinates as written, including N/S/E/W:
Platform asserted by source:
Evidence status:
Source ID, exact URL, and article/section/table locator:
Access date; archive URL and snapshot date if applicable:
Corroborating and conflicting source URLs:
DS test: ROM/version/hash, emulator or hardware, save/event state, actions, result:
Screenshot filename / manifest entry, if relevant:
Open questions and next verification step:
```

Do not interpret walkthrough ordering alone as proof of a required prerequisite. Preserve uncertainty about randomized locations, event flags, prices, translation names, and hemisphere notation. Do not overwrite a conflicting coordinate with a preferred value without explaining why.
