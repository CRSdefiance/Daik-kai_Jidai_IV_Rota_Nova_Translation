01. HOW TO USE THIS GUIDE                                       [ABOUT]
========================================================================
Each walkthrough stop names the town and facility, then tells you
what to do there. Crew and item names appear in the quick-reference
sections too, so you can look them up without retracing the whole route.

Watch for MISSABLE warnings. These come before choices that can cost
you a crew member or item. Keep a separate save before a major story
decision, and avoid overwriting it until you are happy with the result.

The guide contains spoilers for destinations, recruitment, and rewards.
It keeps the story explanation brief so you can enjoy the conversations
in the game. You can explore between the listed stops; their numbering
does not mean that every scene must happen in exactly that order.

References such as [S1] point to the sources at the end. The route is
based primarily on DS-specific published guidance. Remaining uncertain
requirements are noted where they might affect your next move; a full
full DS playthrough is not claimed.

01.2  CHOOSING YOUR FIRST CAPTAIN
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rafael is the clearest first-play route: his Lisbon opening teaches
the systems, and the DS walkthrough gives a detailed recruiting and
story path. Begin with section 03, then follow section 04. [S1]

Hodram begins in Stockholm with a naval campaign and an early fight.
Read 08.1 before setting out; recruiting and preparation matter more
than immediately chasing every northern enemy. [S3H; S2HN]

Lil begins in Amsterdam. Trading, town projects, and keeping the
right companions available are central to her route. Start at 08.13;
her story decisions can change the next journey as much as her money.
[S3L; S2LN]

Maria begins in Hangzhou and follows a westward crew circuit with
several remote voyages. If she is not listed at captain selection,
the mixed-version wiki's unlock route is to finish Rafael, Hodram,
or Lil first. The retained DS help describes three initial choices.
Keep your completed progress, then revisit captain selection.
Use 08.25 for her opening. [S3M; G27]

01.3  KEEP A SMALL VOYAGE NOTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before leaving a region, note the next town and facility, required
companion, quest cargo, and story objects still needed. Check the
actual job text and calendar if there is a deadline. The route stop
numbers are a quick way to return to the relevant instructions.

Keep an earlier save before a major pact, a one-time item question,
or a voyage to a distant settlement. Save again after confirming the
reward or completed story stage. A map, gift, and cargo delivery are
different preparations; use the reference tables when a name is unclear.

02. NINTENDO DS VERSION NOTES                                  [VERSION]
========================================================================
The DS and PSP releases share the Rota Nova name, but their treasure
hunts differ. The version-comparison wiki reports these changes [S3A]:

  - Added treasures: 10 on DS versus 23 on PSP.
  - DS awards the added treasures at their locations. PSP acquisition
    can involve a monster battle.
  - The wiki describes DS sharks as remaining nearby, while PSP sharks
    charge toward ships.

The same source lists these shared Rota Nova changes. Individual DS
details still need checking [S3A]:

  - Naples and Menado are absent compared with old PK.
  - Golden Routes and home-base points are introduced.
  - Settlement passwords allow settlement trade.
  - Goods stop at grade 1; old PK branded goods are absent.
  - Some ship prices and performance differ from earlier versions.

If you are using another guide alongside this one, check its platform
before following treasure coordinates or ship recommendations. DS
button and touch instructions are in section 07.

02.1  OLDER HELP DETAILS THAT NEED A VERSION CHECK
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Some help text retained in the DS game describes older protagonist
bonuses. Rafael's help entry names doubled discovery influence,
while the DS walkthrough describes guild-request money and share
benefits. This guide does not promise the older discovery multiplier
as a confirmed Rota Nova rule. His 20% military-investment discount
is also reported by the DS walkthrough. [G27; S1]

Hodram's help entry describes national battle rewards and doubled
battle influence, but does not establish keeping captured hulls.
The mixed-version wiki labels prize retention PK-only. Keep ship
capture, taking captured gold/cargo, and retaining the hull separate;
none proves the others automatically. [G27; S3H]

Use the current event, reward message, and version-qualified advice
before budgeting around a special bonus. Old help wording is still
useful evidence, but this is one place where the release differences
matter to the actual strategy.

03. GETTING READY: RAFAEL'S TUTORIAL                              [START]
========================================================================
FIRST-PLAY RECOMMENDATION: accept Julio's preparation tutorial.

  "Ask him."        Take the guided preparation tutorial.
  "Prepare alone."  Prepare without his guidance.

Choose "Ask him." if this is your first voyage. Julio walks you through
preparation rather than leaving you to find the commands yourself.
Recruit sailors, then assign them to the flagship while you have only
one ship. Finish those preparations before heading out to sea.

!! RECRUITMENT WARNING !!
Keep the original ship, CARTEIRA, until Cesare joins. Selling it early
can block his recruitment [S1; S3]. Keep her original name too. There
will be time to change your fleet after Cesare is safely aboard.

04. RAFAEL WALKTHROUGH                                          [RAF01]
========================================================================
Rafael's first priority is to build a crew. This route takes you east
through the Mediterranean, then south into Africa before a northern
recruitment detour. Keep Carteira until you reach Alexandria. Opening
and Africa progression follow the DS walkthrough [S1]; supplemental
conditions from the mixed-version wiki are identified separately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before returning south, make a circuit of the northern ports to collect
more crew. The stops below follow the DS route [S1, Africa section].

R11  LONDON: Christina joins on arrival.
R12  AT SEA: meet Lil, then visit Amsterdam for her event.
R13  LEAVE AMSTERDAM: a pirate attack occurs without a battle.
R14  HAMBURG: choose "No need to defeat him." for Gerhard now.

Choose the immediate branch instead of relying on a later visit;
the level threshold for the alternative has not been established.

04.6  THE AFRICA CAMPAIGN
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The commission is to take Espinosa's markets and capture him. Save
before committing to the campaign, and check your ships and crew.
A reliable fleet setup for this battle still needs to be established.

R19  Engage Espinosa at sea for a conversation [S1].
R20  After his faction dissolves, enter an African port, then Sofala.
     WARNING: your Sofala share becomes zero; Silveira receives 50%.
     The DS source also reports a 3,000-gold payment [S1].
R21  Defeat Silveira's faction; enter Sao Jorge for Lower Stone Tablet.

The mixed-version wiki describes declaring war, initial assistance
from Silveira, and his later takeover [S3R, Africa 2]. Treat that battle
assistance as an unconfirmed DS detail. Continue until the faction
actually dissolves; do not assume one naval victory finishes the job.

04.7  SAO JORGE: THE UPPER TABLET
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R22  Tavern: obtain the pirate lead; defeat the pirate privateer fleet
     for Goryeo Incense Burner; give it to the tavern woman [S1].
R23  Enter the opened ruins for Upper Stone Tablet; visit the departure

There is also a guild-task route into these ruins. Its DS requirements

04.8  SOFALA SIDE VISIT AND AFRICA'S PROOF
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R24  SOFALA GUILD -> CEYLON PURSUIT -> SOFALA RUINS [S1]
     Capture the requested target in Ceylon, then complete the
     Sofala Guild job. The opened ruins are in Sofala: Zimbabwe
     Temple. Return there for the Vepar Halberd story and figurehead

R25  At the figurehead choice, take "Exactly" for the item [S1].
R26  Follow the proof map to obtain Aksum Gold Seal [S1].

R24-R25 are grouped as a side visit; their necessity for R26 is not

Read the location from your own map rather than using a guessed
coordinate. Search controls are in 07.10. Confirm Aksum Gold Seal
in inventory before marking this regional objective complete; a ruin
discovery and possession of the regional proof are separate rewards.

04.9  INDIAN OCEAN: UDDIN'S INVITATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
You have two objectives here: build enough influence to receive the
Evergreen Lotus Leaf, and obtain Kushan Platter from Basra's ruins.
The DS route below follows [S1, India]; extra conditions from [S3R]
remain unconfirmed for DS.

R27  SOCOTRA -> DEPARTURE OFFICE
     Accept Uddin's invitation with "No reason to refuse."
     Travel to Basra's guild, then stay at the inn as directed.

R28  BASRA -> TAVERN
     Recruit Al. Keep him aboard for the coming ruins visit.

R29  BASRA -> GUILD
     "Accept alliance." brings Uddin's support and shares, but
     also hostility with Nagalpur. Refusing is another route [S1].

Think about this pact before choosing. It changes the political
situation, not just the next conversation. The mixed-version wiki
warns that Uddin can expand far enough to become your main rival for
regional influence [S3R]. Do not assume an ally will leave every
market open for you.

04.10  CALICUT: CREW AND THE EARRINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R30  CALICUT -> SQUARE
     Recruit Samuel. With Christina aboard, pursue the earrings
     conversation and pay 9,000 gold for Ceramic Earrings [S1].

!! MISSABLE: EARRINGS PURCHASE !!
Bring the money before you begin. The DS walkthrough warns that
continuing to the price and rejecting it can lock out the item.
Save before speaking to the seller. Do not confuse that refusal with
ending the earlier conversation, which the source says leaves a guild
purchase available. See section 06 for the Japanese choice labels.

R31  INDIAN OCEAN -> BUILD REGIONAL INFLUENCE
     Become the leading power, then enter a regional port to receive
     Evergreen Lotus Leaf from Uddin [S1].

04.11  BASRA: RUINS AND THE CURSE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R32  BASRA -> TAVERN / GATE
     Give Ceramic Earrings to Safia. At the opened gate, go around
     to the left; turn the mechanism right for Starlight Globe [S1].

The DS guide warns that pushing the mechanism or turning it left
loses the reward. Save before this choice. Exact English option
labels still need matching; use the direction rather than a guessed
menu quotation.

R33  ANOTHER REGIONAL TAVERN -> RETURN TO BASRA
     Hear that Basra's tavern keeper needs you. Visit him, investigate
     the ruins, and return to the tavern after meeting the curse [S1].

The wiki lists regional influence of 2,000 for the summons [S3R].
That threshold is not yet established for DS. If the lead is absent,
check your progression and influence rather than treating the event
as lost.

R34  BASRA -> GATE
     Bring Arabian Nights and one cargo unit of frankincense to
     obtain Kushan Platter. Keep Al in your fleet [S1; S3R].

If you did not buy the book earlier, return to Istanbul; the listed
price is 2,000 gold [S1P]. Frankincense is sold in Sofala, Mombasa,
and Mogadishu [S1G]. Carry it as cargo for the ritual.

R35  BASRA -> DEPARTURE / INN
     Follow the departure event, then visit the inn with the leaf
     and platter for Solon Leaf Map. Follow it to Rigveda [S1; S3R].

These are two separate acquisitions: the leaf comes from regional
leadership, while the platter comes from the curse event. Check both
items before trying to complete the map sequence.

04.12  OPTIONAL CALICUT RUINS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R36  CALICUT -> TAVERN / GATE
     Give Black Glass Bowl to the tavern woman. In the ruins, feel

04.13  NEW WORLD: PICK UP TWO MORE COMPANIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your main targets here are Ceremonial Knife and Sun-Crest Sheath.
Collect the local companions along the way, and begin Pensacola's
development without expecting every town event to finish on the first
visit. Regional progression follows the DS route [S1, New World].

R37  SANTO DOMINGO -> SQUARE
     Obtain Rainbow Parrot.

R38  VERACRUZ -> TAVERN
     Play Fernando's coin game to recruit him.

THE COIN GAME
Each player takes one, two, or three coins; whoever takes the last
coin loses. Aim to leave Fernando 1, 5, 9, 13, and so on. Once you
control that sequence, make your move complete a total of four coins
with his last move: if he takes one, take three; if two, take two;
if three, take one. He eventually receives the lone losing coin. [G3]

For example, leave nine. After he takes two, take two yourself and
leave five. If he then takes three, take one and leave one. If your
starting turn already has a multiple of four plus one, you cannot
force this pattern against perfect play; wait for an opening rather
than blindly removing the same number each turn.

R39  HAVANA -> ENTER PORT / GUILD
     Recruit Charles, then take the Tlaloc Knife search lead.

04.14  HAVANA AND VERACRUZ RUINS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R40  VERACRUZ -> TAVERN / GATE
     Follow the ruin lead for Jade Jewel. At the bats, going around
     costs a day; going straight through tires the sailors [S1].

Preparation check: Aztec Pictorial Map is sold in Havana for 2,000
gold and is the published map for the Veracruz ruins [S1P; S3I].
Silvia's listed gift is Supreme Loupe, sold in Stockholm for 10,000
[S1B; S1P]. The DS chart reaches this gate through the tavern lead
without specifying that gift step. Check the active lead and held
map before paying for a gift you may already have supplied.

R41  HAVANA -> SQUARE / GATE
     Bring Tlaloc Knife; revisit the square. Enter the opened gate
     with Ancient City Map to obtain Ceremonial Knife [S1].

Buy Ancient City Map in Veracruz for 1,000 gold [S1P]. Keep the knife
after this visit; it belongs to the regional proof chain rather than
ordinary disposable loot. Follow the guild's actual search clue for
Tlaloc Knife; its source-listed coordinate is in section 12.3.

If the guild lead is absent, the wiki lists regional influence of
1,000 [S3R]. That exact threshold remains unconfirmed for DS. Do not
assume the search is permanently unavailable after one quiet visit.

04.15  PENSACOLA: START A TOWN, THEN RETURN LATER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R42  PENSACOLA -> ENTER PORT

R43  PENSACOLA -> RETURN WITH CARGO

04.18  NORTH SEA: RETURN FOR THE REGIONAL PROOF
------------------------------------------------------------------------
You already visited the northern ports for crew. This return trip

04.19  THE STONE CIRCLE INVESTIGATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R50  LONDON -> DEPARTURE OFFICE / TAVERN / DEPARTURE OFFICE
     Follow the investigation conversations, then return through
     the gate to obtain Old Parchment [S1].

THE SHADOW CLUE
The dialogue points to a day when daylight and darkness are equal:
an equinox. The pillars' shadows reveal where to look. Continue the
investigation and the crew's deductions; they discuss calculating
the equinox shadow from latitude. You do not need to wait
for an invented March or September date. [G4]

R51  LONDON -> GUILD
     Finish the report for 10,000 gold and 1% London share [S1].

R52  LONDON -> DEPARTURE OFFICE
     Obtain Thales Paper Map. Keep Red Dye and Old Parchment during
     the map events; do not sell an object because it looks blank.

R53  FOLLOW THALES PAPER MAP
     Find Ullr's Bow at the location shown by the map [S1].

Ullr's Bow is this region's proof, not an ordinary weapon to compare
against the sword ratings. Check the Proof category in your inventory
before treating the North Sea objective as complete. [G1]

04.23  JAPAN AND THE KURUSHIMA CONFLICT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R62  OSAKA -> ENTER PORT

Prepare before this arrival. Recruitment here changes your political
position, so treat the visit as more than a harmless crew stop. Save

R63  OSAKA / NAGASAKI -> INNS
     With Jam, obtain Orca Figurehead in Osaka. With Yukihisa,

     Complete the faction conflict; return to Osaka for the lead
     back to Hangzhou [S1].

A meeting at sea produces a battle scene. Keep working on the faction
objective until the dissolution event is actually reached; one fight
alone is not established as enough to finish the entire sequence.

R65  HANGZHOU -> ENTER PORT / TAVERN / GATE
     Receive Bamboo Assembly Plan and recruit Julian. In the ruin
     danger choice, fleeing is the safer branch; obtain Huizong Art.

04.24  OSAKA'S RUIN LEAD AND THE FAR-NORTH VOYAGE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
R66  OSAKA -> TAVERN
     Show Cherry Chintz cargo, then return with Wine cargo to open
     the gate [S1]. These are two tavern visits, not a robe gift.

Keep the goods aboard until both conversations are finished. Do not
confuse showing trade cargo with surrendering a unique quest item.
The exact quantity and consumption behavior still need a DS check.

R67  OSAKA -> GATE
     Follow the ruin scene and its far-north settlement clue. The
     DS guide gives returning as the less costly danger choice.

R68  KORFU -> ENTER SETTLEMENT
     Obtain Tang Bamboo Craft [S1]. Coordinates are in section 11.

Prepare supplies before this northern excursion. Keep the assembly
plan too; the translated ritual discusses the bamboo craft as a
device whose use reveals the proof map. [G5]

07. CONTROLS AND GAME SYSTEMS                                  [SYSTEMS]
========================================================================

07.1  OFFICERS, SAILORS, AND ENDURANCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your named officers and the sailors assigned to ships are different
parts of the crew. Recruiting a companion does not replace the sailors
your fleet needs. In Assign Sailors, compare Need, Now, and Max, then
check Endurance before leaving port. Endurance is expressed in days;
it is not the same thing as a ship's durability. [G1]

Use the one-ship flagship option during Rafael's opening preparation.
As your fleet grows, check each ship rather than assuming the whole
fleet is ready because its total crew count looks healthy.

07.2  READING SAILOR INFO
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Field       What it tells you
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Level       The officer's experience level.
  HP          Current hit points.
  Stamina     Physical stamina attribute.
  Agility     Agility attribute.
  Charm       Personal charm attribute.
  Wits        Intellectual ability.
  Spirit      Mental fortitude; different from the current Mood.
  Luck        Luck attribute.
  Health      Current physical condition.
  Mood        Current mental condition.

Keep attributes, skills, and temporary conditions separate. A high
Spirit value does not mean an officer cannot be Upset or Confused.
An item may improve a particular duty without changing every number
on the page. Inspect the equipment's role and Effect before assigning
it, then compare the relevant officer's displayed abilities. [G1]

Skills use the labels Vigor, Command, Sailing, Survey, Finance,
Swords, Aim, Speech, Guile, and Observe. The duel panel uses Fencing
for sword-fighting skill. These are ability labels, not extra crew
members or separate temporary conditions. [G1]

07.3  CREW DUTIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Duty              Job
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Captain           Command of a ship.
  First Mate        Assistance to the captain.
  Strategist        Plans and strategy.
  Purser            Accounts and supplies.
  Surveyor          Surveying and navigation information.
  Sail Handler      Handling sails.
  Helmsman          Steering.
  Lookout           Keeping watch.
  Boarding Leader   Leading the boarding party.
  Gunner            Cannon fire.
  Missionary        Religious duties.
  Surgeon           Medical duties.
  Chief Cook        Preparing food.
  Shipwright        Ship repair duties.
  Animal Keeper     Care of animals.
  Deckhand          General deck duties.

Match Equipment to the duty shown in its item details. Role-specific
equipment has an eligibility check; giving it to somebody with the
wrong duty is not a substitute for placing the right officer at the
station. Known DS-help requirements are listed in section 07.9. [G1]

07.4  HEALTH AND MOOD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Health state     Meaning
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Healthy          No listed physical problem.
  Tired            Fatigued.
  Hurt             Minor injury.
  Wounded          Serious injury; worse than Hurt.
  Dying            Near death.
  Sick             Illness.
  Dead             Death.

Mood can read High, Good, Relaxed, Normal, Upset, or Confused. High
means an elevated mood here. Confused is a mental condition, while
Hurt and Wounded belong to Health. Check both fields after a difficult
voyage or battle rather than judging the officer by HP alone. [G1]

If Health is worsening, make recovery your next priority instead of
extending the voyage for an optional discovery. Use an inn or the
recovery-room assignments in 07.15, then inspect the officer again.
Poor condition can reduce efficiency or stop work; keep essential
stations staffed while an officer rests [G25]. Exact recovery times
and a revival rule for Dead are not established by the cited help.

07.5  WARNINGS AT SEA
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The warning's name tells you what sort of problem or encounter to
investigate. These labels include ordinary weather and discoveries
as well as dangers; not every message is a disease. [G1]

  Crew problems    Scurvy, Epidemic, Mutiny, Confusion
  Ship/supplies    Rat Infestation, Leaks
  Weather         Storm, Blizzard, Rain, Snow, Lightning, Fog, Calm
  Navigation      Reefs, Drift Ice, Tornado, Whirlpool
  Sea encounters  Giant Squid, Monster Fish, Whales, Dolphins, Sharks
  Other sights    Aurora, Sirens, Mermaids, Strange Bubbles

Keep track of supplies and crew condition when a warning appears.
Do not keep sailing simply because an encounter has an interesting
name. If the fleet is in trouble, a nearby port is a better immediate
objective than the next optional item.

07.7  NORMAL SAILING CONTROLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Control       Action described by the DS help
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  D-pad         Steer during normal sailing.
  L / R         Rotate sail angle.
  A while sailing Open/close sails.
  Touch         Sail toward the direction touched.
  Farther touch Increase sailing speed.
  Touch ship    Stop in place.
  X             Open shared commands in town or at sea.
  Y             Enter/cycle the sailing information modes.
  A in Search   Perform the search.

The manual footer's Rotate and Sails labels describe two different
actions: L/R turns the sail angle, while A changes opening. A in
Search is the search action instead. Use the current mode before
interpreting a button press. [G31]

Ships leave port with sails Half Open. Opening them increases speed;
closing them reduces it. With a Sail Handler on every mast, Half Open
provides automatic sail adjustment. These instructions come from
the DS help and sailing panels [G12].

Use the appropriate mode before pressing its action button. The
information mode asks for a ship or city target. Search mode concerns
your own ship. The war mode selects a fleet or city and changes the
political situation; it is not another harmless inspection screen.
The help says you cannot declare war on an allied faction's fleet.

07.8  WHY AUTO MOVE MAY BE UNAVAILABLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Auto Move needs a Captain and Surveyor. Your faction must have share
in both endpoints and every intermediate city on a connected route.
Valid routes appear in red on the Route Map. Discovering two towns
alone is not enough to create that connection. [G12]

Before assuming the command is broken, check:
  [ ] Captain assigned.
  [ ] Surveyor assigned and eligible.
  [ ] Both endpoint cities and the intermediate cities have your share.
  [ ] The path is connected on the Route Map.

Keep a manual-navigation plan for unexplored areas and distant
settlements. A town route does not automatically become a safe path
to every hidden village or treasure coordinate nearby.

STOPPING AUTO MOVE
While automatic sailing is active, press B to open the stop prompt,
then A to confirm. The recorded Riga-to-Stockholm emulator route
returns to manual sailing after this sequence. That observation does
not guarantee that every scripted transition can be interrupted the
same way; use the actual prompt before resuming manual control. [G31]

------------------------------------------------------------------------
The DS help lists these minimum skills [G12; G21]. Skills are separate
from the six base attributes and temporary Health/Mood conditions.

  Duty / room       Required skill

  Animal Keeper     Vigor 130
  Missionary        Speech 160

  First Mate        Speech 100
  Helmsman          Sailing 60

The room help's Captain entry says main character only. The separate

Do not read the flagship room entry as a ban on assigning captains
elsewhere in the fleet, or import another version's Command minimum.

The help describes a Shipwright repairing at sea and lowering yard
repair costs; a Surgeon reducing illness risk and fatigue and enabling
curative items; a Chief Cook slowing food/water use; and an Animal

benefits, not guaranteed prevention percentages or repair amounts.

A Missionary enables the square's goods distribution; a First Mate

and a Purser may improve buying terms. Skill-qualified officers can
still have individual duty restrictions. Check the assignment screen.

07.10  SEARCHING WITHOUT EXHAUSTING THE CREW
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The search panel says you may find water, food, items, or supplies.
An empty search adds 3 sailor fatigue, and searches can also lead to
desertion or animal attacks [G12]. Repeated attempts are not free.

Check coordinates, active item clues, supplies, and crew condition
before spending days searching. If several attempts fail, review the
location and prerequisites instead of assuming enough button presses
will eventually bypass an event lock.

A skilled Surveyor expands the latitude/longitude information visible
at sea. Unknown coordinates therefore need a crew/skill check, not
an invented position taken from a modern map. Exact visibility ranges
and all search success conditions still need additional DS evidence.

Private Cabins and Recreation Rooms provide the recovery benefits
listed in 07.15. Watch the condition fields while resting officers.
The cited help does not supply exact cure times or a complete set
of disease-specific item-consumption rules; use the actual recovery
message rather than assuming a fixed number of days is always enough.

07.11  REEFS AND DRIFT ICE: EQUIPMENT CAN MATTER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The shared DS messages include reef encounters where extra armor
prevents damage, and ice encounters where a ram breaks the ice and
leaves the ship unharmed [G18]. That gives these fittings a voyage
use beyond their combat role. Check the refit before a northern
exploration trip or an unfamiliar coastal leg.

These are specific protected-result messages, not proof that every
reef or ice collision is harmless whenever a fitting is installed.
The exact armor level, hazard checks, and damage rules remain
unresolved. Keep the shipyard's armor-versus-speed tradeoff in mind;
see section 09.9. After an encounter, inspect the actual hull and
crew instead of continuing solely because a piece of equipment was
supposed to protect you.

07.12  CONTRACTS, SHARE, AND EXCLUSIVE PORTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Get your first 1% through a contract at the Palace or Governor's
Office. Market share determines how much specialty stock you can buy;
1% permits selling without a quantity limit. Increase share through
commercial or military investment, introductions, and recommendations.
Keep buying access and selling access separate when planning cargo.
[G21]

At 100%, your faction holds an exclusive contract and controls the
town. The DS help describes monthly tribute and attacks against enemy
factions from controlled towns. Another faction's exclusive town will
not give you an ordinary contract: the help instead directs you to an
introduction, recommendation, or war. Check the town's owner before
sailing there expecting to sign a simple contract. [G21]

Military investment raises Arms; better-armed towns are less likely
to surrender under attack. Influence is regional strength, increased
by town share, victories, and discoveries. A quest asking for regional
influence is not asking for the same number of percentage points in
one town. [G21]

07.13  HOME PORTS AND DIPLOMACY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Home Port Points are bonus share in the faction's home town. Each
flag represents a point, and the bonus equals its number of fleets.
Other factions can take that portion only through battle or schemes.
Reducing home-town share to zero moves the home port elsewhere; it
does not by itself prove the whole faction has dissolved. [G21]

Relations and Friendship are different readings. Relations names the
state, such as Neutral, Allied, or At War; Friendship measures how
friendly the faction is. Tavern schemes and guild diplomatic documents
can change relations. Check both readings when returning to an old
ally after a long absence. [G21]

The DS help says successful Threaten documents can subordinate a
faction and that Threaten requires a Proof of Conquest. A subordinate
will not oppose your fleet according to that help. Exact success
thresholds and tribute formulas remain unresolved; do not turn a
single proof or high friendship into a guaranteed submission. [G21]

07.14  FLEET INFO, PERSONNEL POLICY, AND SAVING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open Fleet Info and select a fleet to inspect its data. Selecting a
regional fleet also lets you change its assigned policy. Recheck that
policy when you return to a region after the story changes your enemy
or objective; a fleet's old assignment may no longer suit your plan.
[G22]

The Naval Battle personnel policy arranges officers for combat and
prioritizes skills useful in battle. It is a starting arrangement,
not a guarantee that every story-required companion or preferred
boarding officer is in the position you intended. Inspect the crew
and equipment before triggering a forced encounter. [G22]

Functions contains save, load, quit, and options. Use it to keep a
checkpoint before a timed request, major pact, or one-time ruin
question. On the town screen, select a building's sign to use its
commands; commands marked City only are limited to cities. A supply
village is not guaranteed to offer the same set of town commands.
[G22]

07.15  HP, VIGOR, AND RECOVERY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
HP falls when an officer is injured, including in duels. At zero HP,
the physical condition becomes Dying. Vigor sets maximum HP and
represents physical robustness; the help also links it to resistance
to illness. Current HP, Vigor, and the Health condition are separate
readings. [G25]

Staying at an inn or assigning an officer to a Private Cabin or
Recreation Room gradually restores HP. A Private Cabin also restores
physical condition. For the epidemic condition shown as Sick, the
help describes a Surgeon using medicine items. Check the officer's
actual condition after recovery rather than assuming one stay has
finished it. [G25]

Upset and Confused Mood reduce work efficiency. Private Cabin and
Recreation Room assignments restore Mood. Poor physical condition
can also reduce efficiency or stop an officer working altogether.
Before a difficult voyage, recheck essential stations after injuries
or illness; an officer's normal skill value is not their whole
readiness check. [G25]

The original help lists six physical states; the English status
catalog also contains Dead. No revival rule is established here.
Do not treat ordinary cabin recovery as a tested way to revive an
officer whose status has actually become Dead. [G1; G25]

07.16  WHAT ACTUALLY ENDS THE GAME?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The original DS help lists three game-over conditions [G27]:
  - Your faction's share reaches zero in every town.
  - Every ship in your active fleet sinks or is captured in battle.
  - Sailors reach zero across every ship in your active fleet.

Losing the flagship battle and losing the whole fleet are different
situations. Likewise, losing one town's share is not the same as
losing every contract. Keep reserves, crews, and your remaining ports
in mind before risking the last ship or spending the last voyage's
supplies. The help supplies no special recovery guarantee after these
conditions; keep a save from before the campaign becomes untenable.

09.2  BEFORE A BATTLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Save before committing to a scripted fight. Check crew allocation,
equipment ownership, and who will fill the combat duties. Buying a
weapon is only the first step: confirm Equipped By in the item screen.
Figureheads use Equipped Ship, so check the intended ship too. [G1]

A useful equipment comparison starts with the officer and their duty,
not just the highest number in the table. Keep a practical repair and
recovery plan for the return voyage as well as the battle itself.

09.3  CANNON FIRE: YOU POSITION THE SHIP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gerhard's battle lesson says the ship moves as it does at sea and
guns fire automatically when the enemy is in range. Assign an
officer with strong Aim to the gun station; steering and positioning
remain your job. [G13]

The lesson introduces crossing the T. Think of it as a positioning
problem: try to bring your firing side across the enemy's approach
instead of simply charging straight toward contact. The exact ranges
and firing angles still need DS measurements, so watch what the guns
actually do rather than assuming a universal safe distance.

Keep your intended tactic in mind. If you want to stay in a gunfight,
careless close contact can change the encounter into boarding. If you
want to board, firing from range alone is not the preparation step.

09.4  BOARDING: PREPARE BEFORE YOU TOUCH THE ENEMY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The tutorial calls for refitting a hold into Marine Quarters, hiring
more sailors, and assigning a skilled swordsman to lead them.
Boarding begins when the ships make contact. [G13]

Before closing the gap, check:
  [ ] Marine Quarters installed for the intended boarding setup.
  [ ] Sailors actually assigned to the ship.
  [ ] Boarding officer and equipment checked.
  [ ] Health and HP suitable for another fight.
  [ ] Enough supplies left for the voyage after combat.

Use the Swords/Fencing ability and equipment comparison as part of
that check. A high weapon rating is useful information, but the guide
does not turn it into a fixed damage figure or a guaranteed victory.
Keep an earlier save before testing a major story battle.

09.5  HOSTILE WATERS AND ALLIED HELP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The faction help says approaching a fleet you are at war with can
start combat automatically. Hostile exclusive-contract towns can
attack too. Being near an allied fleet may bring assistance, but it
is not a guarantee that help will arrive for every scripted fight.
You cannot attack an allied faction's fleet through the ordinary
declaration command. [G13]

Check the faction information before sailing through a conflict zone.
The safest way to prepare for an automatic battle is before entering
its trigger area, not after the first enemy message appears.

09.6  DIFFERENT PROTAGONISTS, DIFFERENT FINAL FIGHTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rafael's route describes two consecutive Valdes battles. Hodram's
wiki route describes a Pasha encounter against two fleets together.
Those are different planning problems. Save before their departure
or arrival triggers and prepare for the sequence your captain faces.
[S1; S3H]

Maria's Ghost Ship request is described by the wiki as requiring
cannon combat; boarding may remove the target [S3M]. Its exact DS
quest behavior remains unconfirmed, so preserve a pre-hunt save and
the actual request text rather than treating every battle as a chance
to use the same boarding setup.

CHOOSE A PLAN BEFORE THE ENCOUNTER
  Gunfight: staff the gun stations, keep the broadside presented, and
    steer to avoid accidental contact. Check the flagship's hull and
    crew after each exchange rather than watching the enemy alone.
  Boarding: fit Marine Quarters, assign sailors, and prepare the
    boarding officer before closing. Contact starts the fight; it is
    too late to treat refitting as the next step once battle begins.
  Consecutive story fights: provision and recover before the first
    trigger. Do not spend every preparation resource on the assumption
    that the first victory immediately gives you a safe port visit.

These are preparation choices based on the systems above [G13; G24],
not a tested minimum fleet for every captain. Use 09.19-09.21 to compare
hulls, published layouts, and cannon options; check DS's combined
gun/marine room limit before copying an older guide's arrangement.

If a battle goes badly, use what failed to guide the next preparation.
A ship short of sailors needs staffing; an injured boarding officer
needs recovery; a gunship making accidental contact needs a different
approach. Buying a larger hull without addressing those problems can
leave you paying more to repeat the same mistake.

09.7  BUY THE SHIP YOU CAN AFFORD TO SAIL
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before buying, decide what the next voyage is for. A trading circuit
needs room for profitable cargo and enough provisions to finish the
round trip. A recruiting trip needs endurance between unfamiliar
ports. A story battle needs a crew and refit suited to the tactic
you intend to use. Leave gold for those preparations after paying
for the hull; an empty new ship is not a finished upgrade.

Use the actual Ship Info and shipyard quote for your budget. Price,
necessary sailors, maximum sailors, cargo space, and provisions are
separate questions. A maximum sailor count is capacity, not the
number currently aboard. After changing the layout, check the new
crew requirement before leaving. Falling below Need slows the ship;
see section 07.1 for the Need/Now/Max display. [G12]

A useful purchase check:
  [ ] What does this ship improve for my next voyage?
  [ ] Can I afford the hull, planned refit, crew, and cargo together?
  [ ] Can I staff the sailing duties needed by the finished layout?
  [ ] Will its provisions cover the longest leg and a return reserve?
  [ ] Is replacing this particular ship safe for the current story?

For Rafael, keep Carteira until Cesare's upgrade event has happened.
That is a story dependency, not a reason to keep every starter hull
forever. Check the route before selling it. [S1]

09.8  REFITTING: LOOK AT THE WHOLE QUOTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The shipyard shows Cost, Refund, and the time required before asking
whether to proceed. Removing equipment can produce a refund, while
help from officers can reduce the refit bill by a displayed amount.
Neither is a fixed discount promised for every visit. A refit takes
days: check the calendar first if your next clue or appointment is
timed. Reset changes before committing if the plan no longer fits
your budget. [G15]

Do one planned refit for the next job. Buying a trade hull, fitting it
for combat, then immediately turning it back into a cargo carrier
makes each new purchase harder to evaluate. Compare the finished
layout and its quote before accepting, rather than comparing hull
names alone.

09.9  SAILS AND ARMOR: WHAT THE DS DESCRIPTIONS SAY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Component          Described purpose
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Square sail        Favors sailing with the wind.
  Lateen sail        Favors sailing against the wind.
  Topsail            Adds performance with the wind.
  Staysail           Adds performance against the wind.
  Split Sail         Adds speed with the wind.
  Jigger Spanker     Adds speed against the wind; large ships.
  Extra armor        Adds strength but slows the ship.

The placement descriptions also restrict combinations: a lateen sail
cannot have a square sail behind it, and a square sail cannot have a
lateen sail ahead of it. Use the refit screen's permitted positions
rather than trying to force an arrangement copied from a different
ship or version. A stern position can take an Aft Gun; large ships
can instead use a spanker there. The figurehead has its own mounting
position. Cannon type is changed separately. [G15]

For a voyage with a troublesome headwind, compare the against-wind
options before buying armor. For combat, consider whether the extra
strength is worth the speed change. These descriptions explain the
tradeoff; they do not establish a fastest layout for every wind or
an exact DS speed formula.

09.10  CARGO, PROVISIONS, OR A FIGHTING HOLD?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A provisions room stores water and food. The battle lesson converts
a hold to Marine Quarters for boarding; that decision belongs in the
same plan as hiring sailors and equipping the boarding officer.
Gun-station officers speed cannon fire, and the shipyard description
allows up to four gun rooms. [G15; G13]

DS's version-comparison page also limits Gun Rooms plus Marine
Quarters to five combined [S3A]. Four gun rooms and four marine rooms
are individual ceilings, not permission to fit all eight together.
Check this combined limit before paying for a battle layout; see 09.21.

The DS help limits Cargo Holds to five per ship and Marine Quarters
to four. Supply Holds, Cargo Holds, and rowing rooms do not take named
officers. More supply rooms allow more food and water; more cargo rooms
allow more trade goods. A fitted room still needs its actual supplies,
cargo, or qualifying officer. [G21]

Do not fill every available hold with combat rooms simply because
they fit. Decide how the ship will reach the fight and get home.
Equally, do not enter a forced battle with a layout intended only
for carrying goods. Use the route's battle warning to choose when
to change the ship's job.

09.11  ADVANCED SHIPYARD UNLOCKS: PROVISIONAL LEADS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The mixed-version ship page links better guns and hulls to town
investment. It suggests armament 5,000 for Heavy Cannon and 7,000
for Carronade; these are not verified DS thresholds. Its LineShip
and Ironclad estimates vary by town and include a Switch report.
Treat them as leads, and check actual stock after investment. [S3S]

09.12  LIMITS OF THE SHIP AND BATTLE COMPARISONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
DS catalog hull/base prices are in 09.19; published starting layouts
are in 09.20. Actual refit capacities, unlock thresholds, speed formulas,
cannon ranges/damage, and captured-hull retention still need separate
evidence. No measured DS retreat-input or optimal battle-loadout rule
is established. Use the finished ship's quote and information, with
the version-qualified battle advice rather than a copied HD maximum.

09.13  SHIP INFO: READ THE FINISHED SHIP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hull is current/maximum durability. Equal values mean the ship is
undamaged; a lower current value calls for shipyard repairs. Compare
the current hull before another fight rather than judging readiness
from the original hull type or its maximum alone. [G22]

Water and food are the remaining barrels. Running out sharply raises
crew fatigue. Chief Cooks and Animal Keepers help food last longer,
but that is not permission to leave without supplies. After loading
trade cargo, check provisions and Endurance again. [G22]

Square performance describes sailing with the wind behind you;
Fore/Aft performance describes sailing upwind. Both combine main and
auxiliary performance. The mainmast's sail and equipment determine
the main part; foremast and mizzenmast provide auxiliary performance.
Some hulls lack those extra masts. Compare the finished refit, not
just the number of sails in a picture. [G22]

The crew values are required/current/maximum sailors. Change their
allocation through Assign Sailors. Maximum capacity does not tell
you how many are currently aboard, and a refit can leave your earlier
allocation unsuitable for the next voyage. [G22]

09.14  DUELS AND RAMMING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The DS help says approaching enemy flagships leads to a duel. The
flagship's Boarding Leader takes part; without one, a Deckhand or
the protagonist fights. Equip the officer who is actually assigned
before closing in. A strong weapon on an officer elsewhere does not
prepare that duel participant. [G23]

When your flagship touches an enemy ship, officers also fight, and
their results affect the sailors' boarding battle. The help advises
putting the protagonist in the Captain's Cabin and strong Swords
officers in Marine Quarters and on Deck. Choose the boarding plan
before making contact, especially for a story battle. [G23]

A fitted ram can puncture an enemy when it strikes the enemy's side.
A successful puncture greatly reduces the target's hull, but the
attacking ship also loses some hull. Ramming is not free damage or a
guaranteed puncture. Repair and check your own ship before another
attempt. [G23]

09.15  CITY VICTORIES AND LOSING A BATTLE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A city battle is won when the town's Arms reaches zero or it
surrenders. Lower Arms can make surrender possible. The DS help
states that victory grants 10% town share. This does not mean that
one victory automatically produces a 100% exclusive contract. [G23]

Your fleet loses when its flagship reaches zero hull or is captured.
The help says the protagonist transfers to another fleet ship and
combat ends when the flagship is lost. It does not establish what
happens when there is no remaining ship, nor every story encounter's
consequences. Preserve a pre-battle save. [G23]

Combat actions occur automatically, and crew assignments can be
changed on the Deck screen even after combat begins. Prepare first
so that an unexpected encounter does not begin with empty gun or
boarding stations. High-Aim officers in every gun station increase
the number of shots; Bow Gun and Aft Gun add some forward/backward
fire coverage. Exact angles and damage remain unmeasured. [G23]

09.16  FLEET VICTORY: TARGET THE FLAGSHIP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A fleet battle ends in victory when the enemy flagship reaches zero
hull or is captured. The DS help does not require destroying every
escort first. Judge your next move by the enemy flagship's position
and your own flagship's condition, rather than pursuing a damaged
escort while the decisive target gets away. [G24]

Victory reduces the defeated faction's share in towns near the
battlefield. Capturing a ship may also yield its gold or cargo. That
is not a guarantee of a particular sum, and it is separate from the
question of retaining the captured hull. Do not budget a story voyage
on spoils you have not received. [G24]

The help describes steering as your direct battle action; gunfire
and other attacks are automatic. Hostile fleets, enemy exclusive
ports, and monsters can start combat. Prepare before passing through
those areas. No reliable DS retreat-button instruction is established
here; an unverified escape input is not a safe battle plan. [G24]

09.17  DOCKS, CAPTAINS, AND SPARE SHIPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A dock stores and retrieves ships. The DS help says sailors, cargo,
and provisions can remain aboard a stored ship. When retrieving it,
you can also change formation and sailor assignments. Inspect that
old load before putting a stored ship back into active service; it
may still hold goods you set aside much earlier. [G26]

Appoint companions as captains of ships other than the flagship.
Appointing the protagonist to a ship makes it the flagship, which
must lead the formation. The help says ship captains gain levels more
easily. Consider a companion captain when developing your crew, but
keep the story-required officers available for their event scenes.
[G26]

Workrooms have refit restrictions. Captain's Cabin, First Mate's
Office, wheel, Surveyor station, Lookout, masts, and Deck cannot be
replaced with another workroom type. Except for Private Cabins, masts,
and Deck, the same workroom type cannot be installed more than once
on one ship. These restrictions concern workrooms; Marine Quarters
and gun stations are the separate hold spaces discussed in 09.10.
[G26]

occupied. It is not a measured Auto Move speed ranking. See 09.9 and
09.13 for sail direction, fittings, and the actual performance display.

09.21  CANNON TYPES AND FITTING CHOICES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The English cannon catalog names six types [G35]:
  Saker / Culverin / Pedrero / Cannon / Heavy Cannon / Carronade.

The original DS help says cannon choices increase with town Growth,
and the cannon displayed at the wheel position selects the type.
Use the equipment screen's actual power/quote information when
comparing a change. No measured DS range or damage table is established
here; a longer-range label does not supply a universal safe distance.
[G15; G35]

Bow equipment is a choice among Split Sail, Bow Gun, and Ram. Stern
equipment is a choice between Jigger Spanker and Aft Gun. Choose for
the ship's job: wind performance, firing coverage, or ramming. Do not
budget a standard fitting as though all three bow choices occupy the
same slot together. Extra armor increases maximum hull but slows the
ship; adding a hold also reduces speed according to the help. [G35]

Repairing reduced hull costs gold and days. A Shipwright can reduce
the bill. Include that time in a timed mission, rather than arriving
with exactly enough sailing days and discovering the ship still needs
repairs before the return leg. [G35]

DS HOLD-LIMIT WARNING
The help lists up to four gun and four marine holds individually.
The version wiki explicitly reports a combined limit of five for
PS/DS/PSP, unlike the HD four-plus-four layout [S3A]. That combined
DS limit is source-reported rather than tested here. Plan within it
and check what the shipyard actually permits; do not copy an HD
maximum-guns-and-marines refit into a DS battle plan.

10. TRADE GOODS AND GOLDEN ROUTES                                [TRADE]
========================================================================
10.1  START WITH A ROUND TRIP YOU CAN AFFORD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Buy cheaply and sell where the price is higher. Start with a small
selection of goods and compare town prices rather than filling every
hold with the first expensive product you see. The opening tutorial
teaches this basic rule. [G11]

Keep money for supplies, sailors, repairs, and story purchases. A
shipment can look profitable while leaving you unable to finish the
voyage or pay for an important item at the next stop. Record what
you paid, not just what you hope the destination will offer.

  Simple trading notebook
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Buy port / good / quantity / total paid
  Sell port / quantity sold / total received
  Supply and other costs for the trip
  Story cargo still aboard

As an arithmetic example, buying for 100 and selling for 150 gives
50 before travel costs. It does not give 150 profit. The example is
a calculation aid, not an observed game price or recommended route.

10.2  SHARES, STOCK, AND CHANGING PRICES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The mixed-version trading wiki lists town shares as the requirement
for using a trading post. It also says repeated buying raises that
good's local price, repeated selling lowers it, and goods already
produced by a town tend to fetch poor resale prices there [S3T].

Use those points as planning advice while the precise DS rules are
being checked. Inspect the current buy/sell screen before committing
to a large order. A route that worked earlier may be weaker after
you have emptied its stock or repeatedly supplied the same buyer.

Avoid carrying story deliveries as if they were ordinary spare stock.
Lil's nine spices, Cocoa cargo, and the various town shipments need
to survive your other trading stops. Reserve the required holds first.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
These selling locations come from the DS goods list [S1G]. They are
sources for the cargo, not guaranteed profitable destinations or
promises of immediate stock. Use the labels in the cargo screen.

Plan the list around your existing voyage instead of sailing across

delivery; a partial shipment is not assumed to finish the request.

10.4  GOLDEN ROUTES: DISCOVER, THEN REPORT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A Golden Route is a specially recognized trade opportunity. Watch
for the discovery message after a sale, then follow the guild-report
prompt. Reporting is a separate action from making the profit.
The wiki describes two years of reward payments [S3T].

The DS help says discovery requires a trading profit rate exceeding
500%, then directs you to report to a guild [G20]. The wiki instead
uses a sale-price/buy-price ratio of five or more [S3T]. These are
not identical threshold statements. The DS help does not define the
calculation, so this guide does not turn it into a guaranteed sale
price or claim that exactly 500% qualifies.

Open Info -> Golden Route Records to review routes already found.
Use the discovery message as confirmation before planning around
reward payments. A profitable sale and a recognized route are separate
things; the records screen tracks actual discoveries. [G20]

The wiki ties payment months to stock arrival and says different
goods can qualify separately [S3T]. Treat the actual report and
payment messages as your confirmation, not an assumed monthly total.
Keep a note of each discovery and the guild where you reported it.
The DS help says each discovered route can be reported only once;
reporting grants rewards for a limited period. It does not promise
that repeated reporting renews those payments. [G23]

10.5  TEST SMALL BEFORE COMMITTING THE FLEET
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For a candidate route, compare source stock, destination price,
travel length, and whether you can buy useful return cargo. If the
profit depends on a temporary fashion, allow for it to change before
you arrive. The profitability of a cargo belongs to that transaction,
not just to its name in a guide.

PSP/HD route recipes remain comparison leads, not guaranteed DS money
methods. The wiki even describes passwords as PSP-only in one section,
contradicting the DS-specific password list used here. Use the DS
settlement chapter for access and verify the game's actual recognition.

Use 10.6 for product lists, 10.10 for price conditions, and 10.11 for
trends/investment. Live quotes remain necessary; exact DS Golden Route
recognition and payment formulas are not established by those tables.

KEEP A SIMPLE TRADING LEDGER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Record what you paid rather than relying on a remembered market quote.
For each cargo, note its buying port, quantity, total cost, intended
selling port, and the money actually received. Subtract the purchase
cost from sale proceeds to find the cargo's gross profit. Sailing,
repairs, crew, and further purchases still come out of your funds.

Example arithmetic, not a reported game price: buying cargo for 2,000
and selling it for 3,000 leaves 1,000 before voyage expenses. That
does not establish a Golden Route. Only the discovery message and
Golden Route Records confirm recognition; see 10.4. [G20; G23]

Before replacing a modest circuit with a long ocean crossing, check:
  [ ] Enough cash remains for supplies and repairs after buying cargo.
  [ ] The destination's quote still justifies the voyage.
  [ ] A useful return cargo is available, or the trip serves a quest.
  [ ] Required quest cargo is kept separate from stock you plan to sell.
  [ ] The fleet's Endurance covers your next planned supply stop.

Keep a profitable short circuit available while building shares and
ships. A high selling quote is useful only if the fleet can reach it
and remain funded for the next voyage. Treat distant prices as leads
to investigate, not money already earned.

10.7  CREATE A SPECIALTY BEFORE EXPECTING NEW STOCK
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The DS help says a source item can create a new specialty when you
bring it to a suitable town's market. It will not work in an unsuitable
town or one that already has five specialties. More than one town
can suit an item; the help suggests choosing a convenient town where
you have a high market share. [G20]

Choose the town before handing over the item. Count its existing
specialties and consider whether the new product will fit a useful
route. A production item is different from cargo you buy afterward;
check the market before setting off to deliver the new good.

Stock replenishes in the product's arrival months. Empty stock is
not proof that a specialty has disappeared, and the directory is not
a promise that every product restocks every month. Growth can expand
a town's selection and improve quality. Grades run from 5 to 1, with
1 best; higher-quality goods earn greater trading profits according
to the DS help. [G20]

Section 12.13 pairs the special items with their reported products.
Exact DS town compatibility and each product's development threshold
remain unresolved; do not infer suitability from modern geography.

10.8  MOVING CARGO: DO NOT LEAVE IT OUTSIDE THE SHIPS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The departure port's cargo-arrangement command moves goods between
ships. The original DS help warns that cargo left outside the ships
is discarded. Finish placing every item you intend to keep before
completing the arrangement. [G24]

Before reorganizing for a larger ship or a battle refit, list the
quest cargo you must retain. Move those goods first, then arrange
ordinary trade stock. Check both quantity and product afterward;
buying replacement cargo may require a voyage you did not budget.
A useful refit can still be an expensive mistake if it leaves a
required story delivery behind.

10.9  REGIONAL FLEETS: BUDGET AND CHOOSE A POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Create a regional fleet through the dock. Its companion commander
needs Command at least 150, and each new fleet requires 100,000 gold
in operating funds. The DS help allows up to six regional fleets.
Check your flagship crew before appointing the commander: their
absence can remove a useful station or a required story companion.
[G26]

  Policy purpose       What the original help describes
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Delegate a region    Commander handles trading/investment; monthly
                       balance reports arrive.
  Patrol               Wait near a contracted town against attacks.
  Enter a port         Visit a contracted town.
  Attack a town        Attack a town where you have no contract and
                       reduce the other faction's share.

Choose the assignment for your actual objective. A trade delegation
is not the same order as holding a town against enemies. You can
change policy through fleet information. The help gives no guaranteed
monthly profit, so read the reports before counting on this money to
fund your next ship or town project. [G26]

Reorganizing or dissolving a regional fleet requires it to be docked
in the same town as you. Dissolving returns its commander and remaining
operating funds. If all its ships sink, the help says the commander
returns to the protagonist's fleet. That statement does not promise
that lost ships or cargo are recovered. [G26]

Some treasure clues require a named regional fleet stationed in a
specific cultural area. Keep those conditions alongside your policy
choice; a profitable wandering delegation may not satisfy a clue
that asks for a stationed fleet. See section 12.3.

10.10  READ PRICES IN THE CONTEXT OF THE TOWN
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Town status changes the price environment. The original DS help
and current translated status labels describe the following. [G33]

  Status       Reported price effect
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  Normal       Ordinary conditions.
  War          Weapons rise; other prices fall.
  Plague       Food and medicine rise; other prices fall.
  Famine       Food rises; other prices fall.
  Boom         Prices rise overall.
  Slump        Prices fall overall.
  Shortage     The affected goods category rises.

Compare the actual source and destination quotes before buying.
A product that was profitable on the last trip may face a different
market now. The price-information screen also lists arrival months;
other-town information costs 50-200 gold according to the help. Empty
stock replenishes in its arrival months, not on every visit. [G33]

10.11  TRENDS AND COMMERCIAL INVESTMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Trending goods become expensive. The help says trends can arise
naturally or be encouraged by a Missionary. With that officer, the
square's distribution command gives goods away and may create a
trend in nearby towns. This is a possible market change, not a
certain immediate return on every free distribution. [G33]

Large sales of trending goods may earn free share. In an exclusive
town they may instead earn introductions or recommendations, useful
for contracts that were unavailable through the ordinary method.
A red buying price marks trending goods in the price-information
screen, according to the help. Check the actual product and town
rather than assuming every shortage is the same as a trend. [G33]

Commercial investment accepts 1-20 units, each costing half the town's
Growth in gold. At Growth 1,000, one unit costs 500. The help says share
can rise when total share is below 100% or an enemy at war with you
holds a share. Growth can add specialties and improve quality; it
also affects sailor recruitment and cannon availability. Review the
quote and the town's ownership before investing. [G33]

The original DS trading help explicitly lists Lil's 5% higher selling
price. That is separate from a profitable route's changing market
conditions; still read the quote before loading the ship. [G33]

10.12  AN EARLY EAST ASIA CARGO CIRCUIT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kurogane's Maria opening recommends Quanzhou Silk to Hangzhou, then
Quanzhou Tea to Seoul, continuing through Yizhou and back to Hangzhou
[S2ME]. The DS goods table supports those buying products [S1G].
Prices and grades still vary; treat this as a candidate circuit,
not a fixed-margin money formula.

  1. At Quanzhou, compare Silk's buying price with the Hangzhou
     selling quote. Buy only what your share and voyage budget allow.
  2. Sell at Hangzhou if the quote leaves a worthwhile profit after
     the trip. Keep cash for supplies and your next cargo purchase.
  3. Return to Quanzhou and check Tea for the Seoul leg. Seoul is
     also the square stop for Ifa in Maria's early crew circuit.
  4. At Seoul, complete the intended crew/event visit and compare
     available return cargo before continuing to Yizhou/Hangzhou.
     A recruitment trip can pay for itself without every hold being
     dedicated to maximum-profit cargo.

Later, the same source suggests adding Naha to the Chinese-port
circuit. It describes repeated Naha investment and selling Indian
Wheat, Sulfur, or Honey during a Boom [S2ME]. Check the actual town
status and quotes; the number of investments is not a guaranteed DS
Boom trigger. The DS goods list identifies Indian buying stops in
10.6, and 10.10 explains the status effects.

Start with a circuit you can repeat safely, then expand after hiring
navigation officers and increasing usable cargo space. A long ocean
trip with expensive cargo is a poor replacement for checking whether
the next destination actually wants what you bought.

11.5  EXPLORATION: PLAN STOPS BEFORE YOU LEAVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the settlement coordinates in 11.1 alongside your chart, crew,
and Endurance display. The suggestions below organize known stops
for planning; they are not measured fastest routes or guarantees of
a safe crossing with a particular hull. Settlement coordinates are
DS-source-reported [S1V]. Remote repair and rest remain provisional.

ATLANTIC: REACHING THE NEW WORLD
  Azores, N38 W28, is an optional Atlantic stop. Cod, N41 W70, lies
  farther west and is also used by the alchemy story. Plan the return
  before leaving Europe; reaching the western shore does not mean
  you have enough provisions to search, complete an event, and return.
  A Cod detour is a separate goal from reaching a Caribbean town.

SOUTH ATLANTIC: COASTAL EXPLORATION
  Rio de Janeiro, S22 W43, and Montevideo, S38 W60, give two distinct
  South American targets. Karibib, S22 E14, is on the eastern side of
  the Atlantic. Note the longitude signs: Rio and Karibib share a
  listed latitude but are separated by the ocean. Equal latitude
  does not imply a short voyage.

PACIFIC: MARIA'S CALLAO TRIP
  Callao is listed at S8 W78. Santa Barbara, N34 W120; Hawaii,
  N22 W159; and Tahiti, S14 W147, are possible chart-planning targets
  around the Pacific. They are not an established DS supply chain
  from East Asia to Callao. Check the distances on the actual map;
  do not set out assuming each stop is within your ship's endurance.
  Save and provision before Maria's long western expedition.

EASTERN AND SOUTHERN DETOURS
  Ezo, N42 E141, and Korfu, N60 E165, belong to very different legs
  of a northern trip. Guam, N15 E145, is farther south. Perth,
  S31 E115; Leveque, S16 E122; and Wanganui, S39 E174, are separate
  southern exploration targets, not ordinary nearby port errands.

BEFORE AN UNFAMILIAR LEG
  [ ] Surveyor assigned; enough skill to read the needed coordinates.
  [ ] Need/Now/Max sailor counts checked on the finished ship layout.
  [ ] Endurance checked with the actual crew and supplies aboard.
  [ ] Officer Health, Mood, and crew fatigue suitable for the voyage.
  [ ] Target and return stop identified by both latitude and longitude.
  [ ] Required password or quest item retained.
  [ ] Funds left for supplies and repairs after arrival.

If supplies become tight, stop treating every search as progress.
An unsuccessful search raises sailor fatigue by 3 [G12]. Settle the
clue and position first; the voyage budget must cover failed attempts
as well as the sail home. A supply settlement is not automatically a
shipyard: consult the facility tables before counting on a refit.

KEEP A VOYAGE NOTE
Write the target as a full pair, such as N38 W28, and record the clue
that made you sail there. Note your last supply stop, remaining days,
and intended return stop. For an item search, mark whether the clue
event happened and whether the item is already held. A correct-looking
position cannot replace a missing event requirement.

Separate story trips from optional collection detours. If a quest
requires Callao or Korfu, provision for that destination first; add
other discoveries only when supplies and crew condition permit. After
finding an item, inspect its name before leaving the area. Similar
map names and translated aliases are easier to resolve in port than
after a second unnecessary ocean crossing.

12.15  MYSTERY HUNTING: PIECES, HINTS, AND YOUR OWN MAP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Kurogane's DS/PSP treasure page describes a four-piece Ancient Map
hunt and distinguishes DS guild purchases from PSP map gifts [S2T].
Use the following as source-reported DS procedure, while checking
what your current map record actually contains.

  1. Seek Ancient Map pieces at guilds. Collect the four pieces for
     the hunt; the source gives no complete DS price/eligibility table.
  2. Open Info -> Ancient Maps. Read the map and its marked location.
     The source says Y displays a hint; compare that hint with the
     map before deciding which region and direction to sail.
  3. Prepare the Surveyor, provisions, and return stop. Some hints
     describe a location within a region; others direct you out of
     that sea and farther north or south.
  4. Search at your own map's marked position. Kurogane says positions
     are randomized, even when two maps look alike. Another player's
     example coordinate is not a fixed location for your treasure.
  5. Read the obtained item's actual name, type, and effect. Keep its
     map/description with your notes; do not infer the reward merely
     from the location or a familiar island silhouette.

The source reports five DS treasures per playthrough. Its PSP count
is eighteen, while the item wiki reports twenty for PSP; that conflict
is not a reason to change the shared five-per-run DS claim. PSP boss
encounters remain separate from the DS search procedure. [S2T; S3I]

12.17  VEZAS' SCALES: KEEP A PRE-BARGAIN SAVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The mixed-version equipment references place the offer at Alexandria's
square with at least 20% town share and 210,000 gold on hand [S2Q; S3I].
These are reported event prerequisites, not a guaranteed final quote.

For Rafael, the DS walkthrough names Fernando, Carlo, and Ifa aboard
for the Alexandria square event and says the price can be bargained
down to 40,000 [S1]. It does not state the comparison's 20% share or
210,000-cash gates. Keep all three companions available and save before
the offer; the published minimum held cash and final paid price are
different claims, not two prices to silently merge.

Kurogane allows up to five bargains and says a sixth loses the deal,
with an Istanbul guild purchase as recovery. It explicitly describes
a different PSP buy/refuse branch. DS bargaining and recovery remain
untested, so save before the offer and check the actual choices.
Do not spend your remaining voyage funds on an assumed recovery shop.
[S2Q]

13. SECRETS, EXPLOITS, AND BUGS                                [SECRETS]
========================================================================
13.1  CHECK THE VERSION BEFORE TRYING A TRICK
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The version wiki reports that Rota Nova fixes the earlier ghost-ship
bug and removes the rat-catching minigame [S3A]. It does not provide a
reproducible DS exploit list. No tested duplication or unlimited-gold
trick is established here.

Its old-PK and HD bug lists belong to those releases. In particular,
the repeated debt deduction, HD guild-item disappearance, and HD
monster encounters at unrelated treasure coordinates are not shown
as DS bugs. Their published recovery advice is not a DS guarantee.

13.2  KEEP A WAY BACK BEFORE MAJOR EVENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Keep a separate save before giving up an item, accepting a decisive
pact, attacking a story rival, or entering a timed event. The missable
checklist in section 06 and each route identify the known decision points.
If an event does not fire, check the officer aboard, town share,
proofs held, calendar day, and required facility before treating it
as a bug. Several treasure clues depend on a regional fleet being
stationed in a cultural area rather than on your flagship visiting
that area. See 12.3 for those conditions.

13.3  TRAINING THE CREW DURING USEFUL VOYAGES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The DS help says sailing experience raises levels and that ship
captains level more easily [G25; G26]. Use recruiting, trading, and
item-collection trips to build the crew before a difficult campaign.
Assign companion captains to spare active ships when that fits your
fleet, and check essential duty skills after they grow.

Kurogane describes long-voyage training and reports large level gains,
but its figures are mixed-version examples [S2FAQ]. They are not a
DS guarantee for a fixed number of days. Watch supplies, fatigue,
Health, and Mood while training; an idle ship can still consume what
the voyage needs. Keep a Surgeon and useful recovery rooms available,
and stop the exercise when your actual crew condition calls for port.

A practical target is qualification for the next job: Survey 120 for
navigation, Observe 150 for Surgeon, or Command 150 for a regional
commander. Recheck the current displayed skill rather than chasing a
published level number that may not match your crew's growth.

13.4  LENDING SPARE ITEMS FOR MONTHLY INCOME
------------------------------------------------------------------------
At your home port's Palace or Governor's Office, lend held items to
the ruler. The original DS help says rent arrives monthly according
to item value. Proofs, Proof Maps, and Ancient Maps cannot be lent.
Retrieve loaned items at the home port through the retrieval command.
[G32]

Spare equipment can earn money while you use a different item. Keep
anything needed for the next clue, exchange, or officer duty with
you; retrieving it means a return to the home port. The help does not
give a fixed rent percentage, so count the actual payments before
building a budget around them.

13.5  WHEN AN EVENT DOES NOT APPEAR
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Check the named officer is with your active fleet, the required item
is actually held or unequipped as requested, and the town/facility
matches the event. A visit to a town is not always a visit to its
inn, square, or guild. Kurogane explicitly says PSP can trigger some
events on arrival where PS/DS need a facility visit. [S2FAQ]

For Rafael's Christina lead, the FAQ names an earlier African inn
scene involving Arcadius as a DS prerequisite. The DS walkthrough
also gives a Cape Verde trading-post secret scene. If the London
recruitment does not happen, revisit the inn lead instead of assuming
that the trading-post conversation satisfied every flag. These
reported scenes are not proven to be the same event. [S1; S2FAQ]

For English settlement answers, use the complete word in 11.1. The
FAQ's one-character shortcut explicitly concerns the Japanese DS
release; it does not establish a matching English-input shortcut.
Keep the exact answer and revisit instructions from this guide. [G2]

TROUBLESHOOT IN THIS ORDER
  1. Check inventory: the correct quest object, trade cargo, map, or
     gift must be available. They are different kinds of possession.
  2. Check the active fleet: a recruit commanding elsewhere may not
     satisfy a companion requirement for the flagship's event.
  3. Check the facility and sequence: a guild report can be required
     before a ruin reward or a departure-office map handoff.
  4. Check the stated influence/share/date condition and your last
     completed story stage. Use the qualification beside that event;
     do not treat a mixed-version number as an observed DS fact.
  5. Compare your earlier save before trying an irreversible shortcut.
     Destroying a rival or giving away another object can remove a
     branch you were still trying to investigate.

If the published sources conflict, keep the last successful scene in
your notes and use the route's explicit alternatives. Repeating an
unrelated delivery or searching the same coordinates indefinitely
does not establish that the missing prerequisite has been satisfied.

14.2  SKILL, ATTRIBUTE, CONDITION, OR SHIP VALUE?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stamina, Agility, Charm, Wits, Spirit, and Luck are the six character
attributes. Vigor, Command, Sailing, Survey, Finance, Swords, Aim,
Speech, Guile, and Observe are skills. A room requirement such as
Survey 130 refers to the skill shown in Sailor Info. Health describes
an officer's current physical condition; HP is a separate value.
Endurance is the fleet's sailing time in days, not hull durability.

Older help wording may call Vigor "Health," Speech "Social," and
Guile "Tactics." Read those as the skill names when the instruction
gives a numerical duty requirement. The duel panel uses "Fencing"
for sword-fighting ability. [G1; G12]

14.3  THREE DIFFERENT KINDS OF MAP
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A ruin map is a purchasable item used for a ruin visit. A regional
proof map belongs to the story chain for that region's proof. Ancient
Maps belong to Mystery Hunting. Obtaining one kind does not establish
that you have the other two. Check the exact inventory label before
sailing to a clue. See sections 12.4-12.7 for their separate references.

14.4  NAMES THAT OFTEN LOOK DIFFERENT IN OTHER GUIDES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Rafael: older English sources may use Raphael.
  Hodram: retain this name when matching the Japanese protagonist.
  Maria Huamei Li: the guide uses Maria for short route references.
  Camille: older local text may use Kamil.
  Cherry: Osaka's tavern character, Japanese Sakura.
  Seoul: the English port label for Japanese-source 京城 (Keijo).
         The coordinates refer to the game's location, not a modern-map
         replacement. [G37]

Town, item, and officer together make a more reliable match than an
English spelling alone. Do not change a coordinate, requirement, or
protagonist branch simply because another edition uses a different
name. Exact uncertain choices remain qualified beside their events.
